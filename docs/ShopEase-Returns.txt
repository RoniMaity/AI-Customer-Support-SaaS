ShopEase E-commerce Documentation

Return Policy:
We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have original tags attached.
To initiate a return, log into your account, go to "Order History", and click "Return Item".
A flat $5 return shipping fee will be deducted from your refund unless the item was defective.
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item.

Shipping Details:
- Standard Shipping (5-8 business days): $4.99 or FREE for orders over $50.
- Expedited Shipping (2-3 business days): $12.99.
- Overnight Shipping (1 business day): $24.99.
Orders placed before 2 PM EST ship the same day. We currently only ship within the United States and Canada.

Payment Issues:
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Apple Pay.
If your card is declined, please check the billing address matches exactly what your bank has on file.
For "pending" charges on failed orders, the authorization will drop off your bank statement within 3 business days.
